Hello, I'm Tanvi

I've always cooked for family and friends, and food has been a deep interest for as long as I can remember. More than anything, I love bringing people together, and food is the perfect way to do it.

I studied electronics engineering in Mumbai, then turned that love into Usual Curries, a cloud kitchen I ran on my own, handling everything from the menu to the money.

That took me to culinary school in Sofia, and from there into hotel and restaurant kitchens in Bulgaria, the Netherlands and the United States. Today I'm a sous chef at Vanamo Global Eats and Caffeinary in Bangalore.

I care about clean plating, tight mise en place and a kitchen that stays calm through service.
